What is Sandbox?

A professional network, an incubator and a case competition — open to everyone.

Incubators are selective and time-boxed. Collaborators, mentors and templates are scattered across group chats and blogs. And without a track record, early founders struggle to stand out. Sandbox brings the people, the tools and the proof together in one open place.
