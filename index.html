What is Sandbox?

A professional network, an incubator and a case competition, open to everyone.

  • Incubators pick a fewOpen to every founder
  • Help scattered across group chatsPeople and toolkits in one place

How Sandbox works

From a first idea to a team that ships.

01 · MORI Network

Find your people

Founder and specialist profiles, declared startup pages and intent-based matching to find co-founders and collaborators.

02 · Projects & toolkits

Build your idea

Plan your project with the people you connect with: one board for every task, owner and due date, with toolkits and guidance from experienced founders.

03 · Sprints & track record

Earn the spotlight

Online sprints, peer reviews and sprint badges build a visible track record. Leaderboards and showcases come next.
