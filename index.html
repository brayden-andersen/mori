Declare your startup and get your launch path

Put your idea in a form a specialist can judge in two minutes: a one-page problem brief, the hours you have and the role you need. Then see what your business will need at each stage.

Know what your business needs, stage by stage

Tell us what you’re building and get a launch path for it: the structure, registration, licences and team agreements Indonesia asks for, in order. Networking and the first stages are free; the full path comes with membership. Guidance, not legal advice: licensed partners handle the regulated work.

Sharpen your idea in sprints

Team up for two-week online sprints. Ship real proof, and every sprint your teammates confirm goes on your verified work record.

Specialists can show they’re open to join or co-found an early-stage venture. Founders can show the role they need. Intent-based matching makes the introduction. Your verified work record counts even if a startup doesn’t continue.
