Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move up or down the ladder whenever you like; your projects, documents and connections stay as they are.

Is there a free plan?

Explorer is free for as long as you need it: the MORI Network, sprints and your work record, one project with its Mainframe and tools, and the first stages of your launch path.

How will payment work?

Payments aren’t open yet. When they are, you’ll be able to pay monthly or yearly in rupiah, and cancel at any time.

Can I pay to rank higher or get noticed?

No. Credibility on SandBox comes only from verified work: sprints your team confirms and rungs you reach with proof. No plan buys visibility.

How do partner prices work?

You pay partners directly, at the member price shown up front. Service partners pay SandBox a referral fee when members buy their services, and we show that next to the price.
