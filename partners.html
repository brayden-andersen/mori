Partners

Partners join by invitation or application, never by sign-up.

SandBox members are founders and specialists. Mentors, investors and service providers work with them as vetted partners: each one is checked and signs our partner agreement before they can see a founder’s work.

Who our partners are

Service providers come first. Mentors and investors join later, once our first cohorts are running.

Three kinds of partners

Service providers
Notaries, lawyers, accountants and tax advisers, at fixed member prices. Joining now.
Mentors
Experienced founders and operators. Later, once our first cohorts are running.
Investors
Angels and funds who meet founders with verified progress. Later.

How vetting works

Every partner is checked, and signs our partner agreement, before they can see a founder’s work.

Before a partner meets a founder

How you join
By our invitation, or through a professional application.
What we check
Your identity, your firm or track record, and one or two references.
What you sign
Our one-page partner agreement: keep founders’ ideas confidential, disclose any conflict of interest, never ask for equity or fees without saying so up front, and follow our code of conduct.
How it stays fair
Founders rate every interaction privately. Partners who break the agreement are removed.

How partners are paid

Members pay partners directly, at the member price shown up front. Service partners pay SandBox a referral fee for members who buy their services, and we say so wherever a price is shown. Mentors and investors never pay to see founders, and founders never pay for visibility.

SandBox doesn’t provide legal, tax or financial advice. Partners are independent professionals, responsible for their own advice.
