Your content
You own what you post. Projects and their documents belong to their teams.
Guidance, not advice
SandBox gives guidance, not legal, tax or financial advice. Partners are independent professionals, responsible for their own advice.
Work records
Verified work records reflect what teammates confirm. SandBox doesn’t guarantee any outcome, investment or job.
Plans and payments
Plans and prices are placeholders, and payments aren’t open.
Code of conduct
Treat other members with respect. No spam. Don’t ask for money or equity through messages.

Privacy policy

What SandBox collects, who can see it, where it’s kept, and your rights.

Your personal data

When you sign in
Your Google account’s name, email address and photo, through Google sign-in.
What you tell us
Your sign-up answers: name, date of birth, region, role, specialty, years of experience, employment, industries, LinkedIn, CV file and languages. Later, your links, and a profile photo and banner if you add them.
Your date of birth
Private. Other members only see your age, and only if you choose to show it.
Who sees your profile
Members who finished signing up see each other in the MORI Network. With profile visibility set to Connections, everyone else sees only your name, photo, role and headline. In Invisible mode you don’t appear in Discover; someone who searches your exact full name sees only your name and when you joined.
Photos and banners
Stored as public images: anyone with an image’s link can open it.
Your CV
Stored privately. Only you and your connections can open it.
Messages
Stored in Supabase, and only the two members in a conversation can read them. They are not end-to-end encrypted.
Projects and documents
Visible to the people on the project’s team.
Your settings
Your settings, pinned projects, light or dark mode and which notifications you’ve read are saved to your account, so they follow you to other devices.
Where it’s stored
With Supabase, in its us-east-2 region (Ohio, United States). Your data is held outside Indonesia.
Your rights
Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP) applies. You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data.

Cookie policy

SandBox uses your browser’s own storage, not tracking cookies.

What’s stored in your browser

Browser storage
localStorage and sessionStorage on your device, for your session, your settings, unsent drafts and light or dark mode.
Sign-in services
Google and Supabase handle signing in, and may store what they need for it.
What we don’t use
No advertising or analytics cookies, and no trackers.
Clearing it
Settings → Account → Reset this browser, or your browser’s own settings.
